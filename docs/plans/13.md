# Plan 13 — Persistence: restart recovery + snapshots via alarms [M3]

> Part 13 of the Sidebery → Chromium MV3 migration.
> Master plan: `docs/migrate-plan.md` — read §5.3 (tab-tree persistence) and the alarms
> part of §5.2.
> Prerequisites: Plans 1–12 (M0–M2). The `sessions.set/getTabValue` emulation on
> `chrome.storage.session` exists (Plan 5).

## Goal

Make tree/panel state survive both SW kills and full browser restarts, and move snapshot
scheduling from `setTimeout` to `chrome.alarms`.

## Ground rules

No upstream `src/` edits — overlays (identical export shapes) or `patches/`.
Chrome ≥ 150 (alarms `persistAcrossSessions` properly supported; 30s minimum period).
**Commit after each completed TODO** (`chromium: <summary>`). Firefox build/tests green.

## Context you need

- Firefox `sessions.setTabValue` survives browser restarts. Our `storage.session`
  emulation survives only SW restarts (that's what `storage.session` is). Therefore on
  Chromium the `storage.local` `tabsDataCache` (which upstream already maintains as a
  fallback) becomes the PRIMARY browser-restart recovery path.
- Read: `src/services/tabs.bg.ts` (search `tabsDataCache`, `cacheTabsData`), the restore
  matching logic (url/index sequence matching against live tabs), and
  `src/services/snapshots.bg.ts` (scheduling), `src/services/windows.fg.ts`
  (`uniqWinId`).
